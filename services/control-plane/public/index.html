Proven, or silent

A reviewer earns its slot by being right, not by being loud. The moment a tool posts a finding it cannot stand behind, a developer learns to scroll past it, and every finding after that is wasted, including the correct ones.

So Cavix ships a hard rule: a finding that cannot be reproduced is suppressed. Not softened, not flagged as low confidence. Suppressed. What reaches your pull request is the set of problems that were made to happen on purpose, in a sealed sandbox, and then made to stop happening.
